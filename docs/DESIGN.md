## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: Hand-drawn nocturnal celestial linework with chocolate borders (2.5px-3px), stipple stardust accents, and interactive dream-chart cartography.
- Material & depth: Cream paper-grain surfaces with hard chocolate offset drop shadows (`3px 3px 0 #5C4033` and `4px 4px 0 #5C4033`), avoiding fuzzy generic shadows or heavy frosted glass.
- Containers & buttons: Chunky rounded corners (rounded-2xl to rounded-3xl), tactile press offsets (`active:translate-x-[2px] active:translate-y-[2px]`), tactile pill filters, and clean SVG badges.
- Layout rhythm: Clear alternation between creative laboratory form controls, full-bleed interactive SVG canvas scenes, paired Atlas narrative modules, and compact Ask Pip dream terminal.

## Animation
- Entrance: Staggered soft fade-slide upward (250ms, ease-out) for dream generation results and scene reveal.
- Interaction: Gentle float on Pip's dream ship (2.5s ease-in-out alternate), subtle stardust twinkle, and 120ms press feedback.
- Scroll / transition: Smooth anchor scrolling to World Atelier with tab switching between scene view, atlas, and saved archive.

## Forbidden
- No generic SaaS dashboard cards with pale gray borders and flat neon gradients.
- No raw emojis in place of vector SVG iconography.
- No simulated live AI when credentials are unavailable; demo mode must be explicitly branded and fully interactive.

## Additional Notes
- All user-visible copy is in English.
- Reduced-motion settings disable ambient canvas floating and particle trails.
